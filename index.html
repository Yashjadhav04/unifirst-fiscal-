UniFirst FY2026 results, decoded

Revenue grew 3.7% to $2.52B, but operating income fell 26.3% to $136.0M. Disclosed merger and ERP costs were $43.7M higher than a year earlier, which is most of the $48.5M drop. Excluding them, Q4 operating margin rose to about 9.1% from 8.3%.
